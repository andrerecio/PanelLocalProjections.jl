PanelLocalProjections.jl

Panel local projections (Jordà 2005): for each horizon $h$, a panel regression of $y_{i,t+h}$ on a shock. The package implements:

  • the fixed-effects (within/LSDV) panel LP, with unit-, time- and two-way-clustered, heteroskedasticity-robust and Driscoll–Kraay standard errors;
  • the split-panel jackknife correction of the Nickell bias of the fixed-effects estimator (Mei, Sheng & Shi, 2026, Journal of International Economics);
  • the cumulative-difference OLS estimator, which avoids the within transformation (Ugarte-Ruiz, 2026);
  • t-LAHR inference for panel LPs with aggregate shocks: time-clustered, lag-augmented, heteroskedasticity-robust, with an HC2 + Imbens–Kolesár small-sample refinement (Almuzara & Sancibrián, 2026).
Status

Version 0.2.0, with panel LP-IV (methods, and a public-data example). The roadmap is in TODO.md at the repository root.

Fixed-effect absorption, the OLS solver and all variance estimators are implemented in the package. Their design takes inspiration from Regress.jl and CovarianceMatrices.jl (Giuseppe Ragusa), FixedEffectModels.jl (Matthieu Gomez), and the Stata commands reghdfe (Sergio Correia) and xtlp (Mei, Sheng & Shi). None of them is a dependency.

PanelLocalProjections

Panel local projections (Jordà 2005): for each horizon h, a panel regression of y_{i,t+h} on a shock, with estimators and inference from

  • Mei, Sheng & Shi (2026, Journal of International Economics), "Nickell bias in panel local projection": the split-panel jackknife (SPJ) correction of the fixed-effects estimator;
  • Ugarte-Ruiz (2026, BBVA Research), "Panel local projections without fixed-effects": the cumulative-difference OLS (CD-OLS) estimator;
  • Almuzara & Sancibrián (2026), "Micro responses to macro shocks": t-LAHR inference (time-clustered, lag-augmented, heteroskedasticity-robust; HC2 + Imbens–Kolesár df).

The fixed-effect absorption, the OLS solver and every variance estimator are implemented in this package. Their design takes inspiration from Regress.jl and CovarianceMatrices.jl (Giuseppe Ragusa), FixedEffectModels.jl (Matthieu Gomez), LocalProjections.jl (gragusa; andrerecio fork), and the Stata commands reghdfe (Sergio Correia) and xtlp (Mei, Sheng & Shi). None of them is a dependency.
